# Naming: Availability Screening Results

## ✅ Decision (2026-08-31)

**SourceKettle** has been selected and the project is now branded accordingly — the repository
directory is `sourcekettle/`, and every plan document, the taxonomy builder and the compliance
engine reference SourceKettle.

`sourcekettle.com` was **verified unregistered** via Verisign RDAP on 2026-08-31. Rationale: it
keeps the "source" concept from the original name while pairing it with **kettle** — a real
chemical-engineering vessel (kettle reactor / kettle reboiler) — which anchors the brand in process
plant rather than in a chemical directory. It is suggestive rather than descriptive, which is a
better trademark position than "ChemSource" would have been.

**Still outstanding before launch** (see §"What I did NOT check" below): trademark clearance in
Nice Classes 1, 35, 39 and 42; Nigerian CAC company-name reservation; defensive registration of
`.com.ng` / `.ng` / `.africa`; social handles. Register the domain first — it was unregistered as of
screening, and five of the sixteen taken names below were registered during 2026 alone, so
availability in this space moves.

---

**Screened:** 2026-08-31
**Method:** Verisign RDAP (`rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>`) — the authoritative registry
for `.com`. A JSON response with `"objectClassName":"domain"` means **registered**; an empty 404
body means **not registered**. A nonsense control (`zqzvqxwkemsource.com`) was queried to confirm
the negative signal reads correctly.

### ❌ `reagentline` — reject despite being available

**Reagent Chemical & Research, LLC** — founded 1959, Ringoes NJ, the largest supplier of liquid
hydrochloric acid in North America, 45 locations, 501–1,000 employees, PE-backed (Wynnchurch
Capital). Identical industry, overlapping goods (HCl, caustic soda). Using "Reagent" as your house
mark would create a high likelihood of confusion in the same class of goods. Domain availability is
irrelevant when the trademark is occupied.

### ⚠️ `kettle*` — legal risk low, perception risk real

"Kettle" is strongly owned in the public mind by **Kettle Brand** potato chips (founded 1978, now
Campbell Soup). That's Class 29/30 (food) — a different class from Class 1 (chemicals) and Class 35
(marketplace services), so the legal risk is genuinely low. But you will spend the first year of
every conversation correcting "like the crisps?" Decide whether that's acceptable.

Also note: **four of the seven available names share one root word.** Treat the kettle cluster as a
single option, not four.

## Recommendation

1. **`kemsura.com`** if you want the strongest trademark and international flexibility. Coined
   marks get the widest protection and there is no existing use to argue with. Cost: you must
   build the meaning yourself.
2. **`offtakechem.com`** if you want instant credibility with industry insiders. "Offtake" is the
   exact word for what your platform facilitates. Cost: harder to protect, and the `chem` suffix
   is generic.
3. **`kettleline.com`** if you want the name to carry the strategy — it says "we sell the line,"
   which is the differentiation in `01-executive-summary.md`. Cost: the crisps association.

## What I did NOT check (do these before committing)

Domain availability is **not** trademark clearance. These remain outstanding:

1. **Trademark registries.** USPTO TESS/TSDR, EUIPO, WIPO Global Brand DB, and — since you are
   Lagos-based — the **Nigerian Trade Marks Registry**. Search Nice **Class 1** (chemicals),
   **Class 35** (marketplace/procurement services), **Class 42** (software), and **Class 39**
   (transport/storage, relevant if you touch logistics).
2. **Company name availability.** Nigerian **CAC** (Corporate Affairs Commission) name reservation,
   plus registries in any other jurisdiction you will trade in.
3. **Other TLDs.** `.com.ng`, `.ng`, `.africa`, `.io` — worth securing defensively once you pick.
4. **Social handles.** LinkedIn company page, X, GitHub org — check as one batch.
5. **Linguistic check** if you will trade in Francophone West Africa (Niger, Benin, Togo, Côte
   d'Ivoire) — confirm the name reads acceptably in French.

**Practical advice:** shortlist three, run a professional clearance search on all three (a
trademark attorney in Nigeria will do this for modest cost), then register the domain **and** file
the trademark before you spend anything on brand assets.
