Pick the production line a plant runs. SourceKettle returns every role that line needs — chemicals, machinery and consumables together — which is the whole point of the taxonomy's second axis.

Compliance envelope

Every check below is evaluated by the same rules engine covered by the 30-test suite. A block outcome has no override — the order simply cannot be placed.

Shipment

Add items, then run the checks.

RFQ & sealed reverse auction

Build an RFQ from your cart or from a whole production line, then collect sealed bids. Ranking is on total landed cost — never on headline price — with supplier tier and performance score as documented tie-breakers.

New RFQ

Price index

Rolling 30-day volume-weighted median landed price. An index is only published with at least 5 independent observations, always discloses its n, and excludes related-party transactions. That discipline is what makes it a reference rather than marketing.

Insights

Substitution, supplier scorecards, replenishment, document vault and spend analytics — the services that make leaving the platform an actual cost.

Substitution finder

Finds stand-ins by CAS, segment, grade, form, storage and DG class. Anything scoring below 40 is withheld rather than guessed.

Supplier scorecards

Recomputed from fulfilled orders — never self-reported.

Replenishment

Usage rate against lead time and safety stock. Suggestions respect each product's MOQ.

Document vault

Every SDS revision is retained immutably; only the current revision raises an alert. Expired documents block shipment.
