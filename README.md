# KleenPulse Africa 🇳🇬 🌍
### On-Demand Uber for Household Cleaning, Warehouse Industrial Decontamination & LASEPA-Certified Fumigation

**KleenPulse Africa** is an enterprise-grade on-demand platform designed for household cleaning, specialized commercial sanitization, cost-reflective industrial warehouse scrubbing ($m^2$), and vector eradication across Lagos, Nigeria, with built-in readiness for Pan-African expansion (Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Rwanda).

Built on the proven **Uber-style revenue-sharing economic model (80% Vendor Payout / 20% Platform Fee)** with a **Customer Escrow Protection Engine**, KleenPulse enforces strict regulatory compliance where **all cleaning and fumigation vendors must be background-checked and cleared by the Nigeria Police Force (NPF / Central Criminal Registry) and accredited by the Lagos State Environmental Protection Agency (LASEPA)** before taking jobs.

---

## 🚀 Key System Capabilities

### 1. 📅 Long-Term Service Contracts & Recurring SLAs (Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly / Bi-Annual)
- **Automated Service Schedules**:
  - 🔄 **Weekly Retainer**: Ideal for corporate offices, clinics, and residential estates (*20% Recurring Loyalty Discount*)
  - 🔄 **Bi-Weekly Retainer**: Ideal for busy households and retail shops (*15% Recurring Loyalty Discount*)
  - 🔄 **Monthly Retainer**: Ideal for comprehensive deep cleaning & steam sanitization (*10% Recurring Loyalty Discount*)
  - 🔄 **Quarterly SLA**: Ideal for LASEPA certified vector fumigation & pest eradication cycles (*12% Retainer Discount*)
  - 🔄 **Bi-Annual SLA**: Ideal for industrial warehouse overhaul & post-rainy season termite barriers (*8% Retainer Discount*)
  - 🌟 **Annual Commitment Bonus**: Extra **+5% Annual Discount** on 12-month commitments.
- **Dedicated Vendor Lock-In**: Customers can lock in their favorite certified technician/crew for the entire duration of the contract.
- **Milestone Escrow Billing**: Payments are released per visit upon customer satisfaction confirmation.
- **Digital LASEPA Corporate SLA Certificate**: Verifiable state environmental compliance contract agreements.

---

### 2. 📏 Cost-Reflective Square Meter ($m^2$) Pricing for Warehouses & Industrial Sites
- **Dynamic Industrial Formula**:
  $$\text{Area Fare} = (\text{Square Meters} \times \text{Unit Rate/m}^2) \times \text{Height Factor} - \text{Volume Discount}$$
- **Economy-of-Scale Tiered Volume Discounts**:
  - **500 – 1,999 $m^2$**: **10% Volume Discount**
  - **2,000 – 4,999 $m^2$**: **18% Volume Discount**
  - **5,000+ $m^2$**: **25% Industrial Scale Discount**
- **High-Bay Ceiling Truss Adjustments**: Scissor-lift aerial platform and safety harness rigs included for 6m - 14m structures.

---

### 3. 🔒 Customer Satisfaction Sign-Off & Escrow Protection
- **Zero Premature Payouts**: 80% vendor payouts remain **locked in Escrow** until the client completes a walkthrough inspection.
- **Interactive Quality Checklist**: Inspect rooms, verify chemical ventilation, and check biohazard disposal.
- **Before & After Proof Photo Gallery**: Lightbox inspection of work photos uploaded by technicians.
- **4-Digit Satisfaction OTP Verification**: In-person security code for walk-through sign-off.
- **Free Immediate Touch-Up Request**: Allows clients to report missed spots and hold funds until resolved.

---

### 4. 🛡️ Strict Police (NPF) & LASEPA Regulatory Compliance
- **Nigeria Police Force (NPF) Character Certificates**: Central Criminal Registry (CRID) background check.
- **LASEPA Chemical Fumigation Accreditation**: Class-A & Class-B licenses; WHO Class-II/III pyrethroids only.
- **Interactive Regulator & Audit Desk**: 1-click Approve / Reject portal for state compliance officers.
- **Digital Cryptographic LASEPA Certificate Generator**: Verifiable proof with QR codes and 90-day anti-pest warranties.

---

### 5. 🚕 Uber-Style 80/20 Revenue Sharing & Instant Payouts
- **80% to Certified Vendor / 20% to Platform & Insurance**.
- **Instant NIBSS Instant Payments (NIP)**: Instant cashout to GTBank, Access, Zenith, Kuda, Moniepoint, and OPay.

---

### 6. 🌍 Pan-African Regional Expansion
Switchable regional configs and currencies for:
- 🇳🇬 **Nigeria (NGN - ₦)**: LASEPA & NPF
- 🇬🇭 **Ghana (GHS - GH₵)**: EPA Ghana & Ghana Police CID
- 🇰🇪 **Kenya (KES - KSh)**: NEMA Kenya / PCPB & DCI Kenya
- 🇿🇦 **South Africa (ZAR - R)**: SAPCA / DFFE & SAPS CRC
